# Link Building Strategies

## Overview
Backlinks remain one of the strongest ranking signals. Quality, relevance, and diversity of the link profile directly impact organic visibility.

## Link Quality Factors
- **Relevance**: Links from topically related sites carry more weight
- **Authority**: Links from high-authority domains (strong DA/DR) are more valuable
- **Placement**: Editorial links within body content > sidebar/footer links
- **Anchor text**: Descriptive, natural anchor text (avoid over-optimization)
- **Follow status**: Dofollow links pass PageRank; nofollow/sponsored/ugc are still valuable for discovery

## Strategies

### Digital PR
- Create newsworthy content (original research, data studies, surveys)
- Build relationships with journalists and editors
- Use HARO (Help a Reporter Out) for expert commentary opportunities
- Newsjacking: tie content to trending news stories
- Launch with outreach to target publications

### Content-Led Link Building
- **Link-worthy assets**: Tools, calculators, interactive content, definitive guides
- **Data studies**: Original research that others will cite and link to
- **Visual assets**: Infographics, maps, charts that earn embeds with attribution
- **Resource pages**: Comprehensive guides that become go-to references

### Broken Link Building
- Find broken links on relevant sites (using Ahrefs, Check My Links extension)
- Create or identify content that can replace the broken resource
- Reach out to the site owner suggesting your replacement

### Competitor Link Analysis
- Analyze competitor backlink profiles (Ahrefs, Semrush)
- Identify patterns: what type of content earns them links?
- Find sites linking to competitors but not to you (link gap analysis)
- Replicate their best link sources where relevant

### Guest Posting
- Write high-quality articles for relevant industry publications
- Focus on value to the audience, not just the link
- Avoid low-quality guest post networks (Google devalues these)
- Include a natural, contextual link back to your site

### Unlinked Brand Mentions
- Monitor brand mentions using Google Alerts, Mention, or Ahrefs
- When you find a mention without a link, reach out requesting attribution
- High success rate since they already know your brand

## Outreach Best Practices
- Personalize every email (reference specific content on their site)
- Lead with value: what's in it for them?
- Keep initial outreach short (under 150 words)
- Follow up once after 5-7 days, then move on
- Track outreach in a CRM or spreadsheet
- Aim for 5-10% response rate on cold outreach
