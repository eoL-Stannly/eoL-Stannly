# PRD: Content Strategy & Production Deliverable

**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-02-12

---

## Deliverable Overview

A content strategy document and/or production-ready content that is keyword-targeted, user-focused, and optimised for search engines while maintaining E-E-A-T standards.

## Deliverable Type

`Content Strategy & Production`

## Required Sections

### 1. Executive Summary
- Content strategy overview in 3–5 sentences
- Key content gaps identified
- Recommended content priorities
- Expected organic traffic potential from new content

### 2. Content Audit Summary
- Total pages audited
- Content health distribution (strong, needs update, thin, cannibalised, retire)
- Top performing content (by traffic and conversions)
- Underperforming content with potential
- Cannibalisation issues identified

### 3. Topic Cluster Architecture
For each cluster:
- **Pillar page** — title, URL (existing or proposed), target keyword
- **Supporting content** — list of articles with target keywords
- **Internal linking plan** — how pieces connect
- **Content status** — existing (update), planned (create), or gap

### 4. Content Calendar
| Priority | Content Title | Type | Target Keyword | Volume | Status | Target Date |
|----------|--------------|------|----------------|--------|--------|-------------|
| 1        | ...          | Blog | ...            | ...    | Create | ...         |

- 3-month rolling calendar
- Prioritised by opportunity score
- Includes content type (blog, landing page, guide, video, etc.)

### 5. Content Briefs (Top 5 Priorities)
For each priority content piece:
- **Target keyword** and secondary keywords
- **Search intent** analysis
- **SERP analysis** — what's currently ranking and why
- **Recommended word count**
- **Content outline** (H2/H3 structure)
- **Key points to cover**
- **Internal linking targets**
- **E-E-A-T requirements**
- **CTA / conversion goal**
- **Competitor content analysis** (what to beat)

### 6. On-Page Optimisation Recommendations
For existing pages needing updates:
- **Page URL**
- **Current title tag** → **Recommended title tag**
- **Current meta description** → **Recommended meta description**
- **H1 recommendation**
- **Content additions/modifications needed**
- **Internal links to add**

### 7. E-E-A-T Enhancement Plan
- Author/expert bio requirements
- Citation and source guidelines
- First-hand experience signals to incorporate
- Trust signal recommendations

### 8. Recommendations
Each recommendation must include:
- **Priority** (High / Medium / Low)
- **Action** — specific content action
- **Impact** — expected traffic/conversion impact
- **Effort** — production effort estimate
- **Owner** — writer, editor, or SME responsible

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Content audit covers all indexable pages
- [ ] Topic clusters align with keyword research
- [ ] Content calendar spans minimum 3 months
- [ ] Top 5 content briefs are detailed enough for a writer to produce content
- [ ] On-page recommendations include before/after examples
- [ ] E-E-A-T plan is specific to the client's industry
- [ ] All recommendations have clear owners and priorities

## Output Formats

- **Primary:** Markdown report
- **Optional:** CSV content calendar, individual brief documents
