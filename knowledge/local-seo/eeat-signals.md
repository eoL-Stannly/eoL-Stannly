# E-E-A-T: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness

## Overview
E-E-A-T is Google's quality framework for evaluating content. While not a direct ranking algorithm, it guides the Search Quality Raters who evaluate Google's search results. Pages with strong E-E-A-T signals tend to rank better, especially in YMYL (Your Money, Your Life) topics.

## The Four Pillars

### Experience
- Does the content creator have first-hand experience with the topic?
- Show real examples, case studies, original data, and personal insights
- User-generated content (reviews, testimonials) can demonstrate experience
- Photos and videos from actual experience add credibility

### Expertise
- Does the creator have the knowledge or skill for the topic?
- Author bios with relevant credentials and qualifications
- Demonstrate depth of knowledge through comprehensive, accurate content
- For YMYL topics, formal expertise (medical degree, financial certification) is important

### Authoritativeness
- Is the creator or website a recognized authority on the topic?
- Build authority through consistent, high-quality content on your topic area
- Earn mentions, citations, and links from other authoritative sources
- Industry awards, speaking engagements, and media coverage help

### Trustworthiness
- The most important factor — can users trust the content and website?
- Secure website (HTTPS)
- Clear contact information and physical address
- Transparent about who creates content and reviews it
- Accurate, sourced, and up-to-date information
- Privacy policy, terms of service, editorial policy

## Implementing E-E-A-T for SEO

### Author Pages
- Create detailed author bio pages with credentials, experience, and links
- Use Person schema markup with sameAs links to social/professional profiles
- Link author pages from every article byline
- Include author's other published works and expertise areas

### Content Signals
- Cite authoritative sources with outbound links
- Include original research, data, and analysis
- Update content regularly with accurate information
- Display clear publish dates and last-updated dates
- Have content reviewed by subject matter experts

### Site-Level Signals
- About page with company history and mission
- Contact page with multiple contact methods
- Customer reviews and testimonials (third-party verified)
- Secure checkout and data handling (for e-commerce)
- BBB accreditation, industry memberships, certifications

## YMYL Topics
Your Money, Your Life topics require the highest E-E-A-T standards:
- Health and medical information
- Financial advice and transactions
- Legal information
- News and current events
- Safety-related information
- Civic and government information
