# Citation Building & Local Link Acquisition

## Overview
Citations are online mentions of a business's NAP (Name, Address, Phone). They are a core local ranking factor and build trust with search engines about a business's legitimacy and location.

## Types of Citations

### Structured Citations
- Business directory listings with consistent NAP in a structured format
- Examples: Yelp, Yellow Pages, BBB, industry-specific directories
- Most impactful for local rankings

### Unstructured Citations
- Mentions of business NAP in blog posts, news articles, or other non-directory content
- Less structured but still valuable for local SEO
- Often come from PR, sponsorships, and community involvement

## Citation Building Strategy

### Step 1: Audit Existing Citations
- Use tools like BrightLocal, Moz Local, or Whitespark to find existing citations
- Identify inconsistencies in NAP across listings
- Find duplicate listings that need merging or removal

### Step 2: Fix Inconsistencies
- Standardize NAP format (exact match across all platforms)
- Update outdated addresses, phone numbers, or business names
- Resolve duplicate listings
- Consistency is more important than quantity

### Step 3: Build New Citations
- Start with core platforms (Google, Bing, Apple, Facebook, Yelp)
- Add industry-specific directories
- Build niche citations relevant to the business category
- Prioritize quality directories with real traffic

### Step 4: Local Link Building
- Sponsor local events, charities, or sports teams (earn .org links)
- Join local business associations and chambers of commerce
- Create locally relevant content (neighborhood guides, local event coverage)
- Partner with complementary local businesses for cross-promotion
- Submit to local news outlets for press coverage

## Monitoring
- Track citation accuracy quarterly
- Monitor for unauthorized changes to listings
- Watch for new duplicate listings
- Report spam listings for competitors appearing at fake locations
