# Google Business Profile Optimization

## Overview
Google Business Profile (GBP, formerly Google My Business) is the cornerstone of local SEO. It controls how a business appears in Google Maps and the local pack in search results.

## Profile Setup & Verification
- Claim and verify the listing (postcard, phone, email, or instant verification)
- Choose the most specific primary category available
- Add all relevant secondary categories (up to 9)
- Complete every available field — completeness correlates with visibility

## Key Optimization Areas

### Business Information
- **NAP Consistency**: Name, Address, Phone must match exactly across all citations
- **Business hours**: Keep accurate, including holiday hours
- **Attributes**: Select all relevant attributes (wheelchair accessible, Wi-Fi, etc.)
- **Service area**: Define for service-area businesses (SABs) instead of showing address
- **Website URL**: Link to location-specific landing page, not just homepage

### Photos & Media
- Upload high-quality photos regularly (interior, exterior, team, products)
- Add logo and cover photo
- GBP posts with images get higher engagement
- Google may use user-submitted photos; monitor and report irrelevant ones
- Video uploads (under 30 seconds) can increase engagement

### Google Posts
- Post regularly: offers, updates, events, product highlights
- Include CTAs (Learn More, Call Now, Book, etc.)
- Posts expire after 7 days (events run until end date)
- Use keywords naturally in post content

### Reviews
- Respond to ALL reviews (positive and negative) promptly
- Request reviews from satisfied customers via direct review link
- Never incentivize reviews (violates Google policies)
- Address negative reviews professionally and offer resolution
- Review velocity and recency are ranking factors

### Products & Services
- Add all products/services with descriptions and pricing
- Use keyword-rich descriptions
- Keep pricing up to date
- Link to relevant pages on your website

### Q&A Section
- Seed with commonly asked questions and answers
- Monitor for new questions and respond quickly
- Upvote helpful Q&As

## Local Pack Ranking Factors
1. **Relevance**: How well the profile matches the search query
2. **Distance**: Proximity to the searcher's location
3. **Prominence**: Online reputation (reviews, citations, links, brand signals)

## Multi-Location Management
- Each location needs its own verified GBP listing
- Create location-specific landing pages on the website
- Maintain consistent but location-specific content
- Use GBP's bulk management tools for large portfolios
