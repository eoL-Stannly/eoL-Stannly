# SOP: Keyword Research & Opportunity Analysis

**Owner:** Craig (SEO Director — Content Strategy)
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-02-12

---

## Purpose

Define the standard operating procedure for conducting keyword research and identifying organic search opportunities for client websites. This SOP ensures consistent, data-driven keyword analysis across all accounts.

## Scope

Applies to all keyword research deliverables including initial audits, quarterly refreshes, content gap analyses, and campaign-specific keyword targeting.

## Prerequisites

- Access to client's Google Search Console (GSC) data
- Client brief describing target audience, products/services, and business objectives
- Competitor domain list (minimum 3 direct competitors)
- Any existing keyword tracking data

## Procedure

### Step 1: Gather Client Context (Triage)

1. Review the client brief and extract:
   - Primary products/services
   - Target markets and geographies
   - Business goals (leads, sales, brand awareness)
   - Existing content inventory (if available)
2. Identify the client's current keyword footprint from GSC
3. Document known competitor domains

### Step 2: Seed Keyword Discovery

1. Generate seed keywords from:
   - Client's website navigation and page titles
   - Client brief terminology
   - Competitor homepage and category page analysis
   - Industry-standard terms from knowledge base
2. Expand seeds using:
   - Autocomplete/suggest data patterns
   - "People Also Ask" question patterns
   - Related searches and semantic variations
3. Aim for 50–200 seed terms per topic cluster

### Step 3: Keyword Data Enrichment

For each keyword, gather:
- Monthly search volume (localised to target market)
- Keyword difficulty / competition score
- Current ranking position (if any)
- SERP feature presence (featured snippets, PAA, local pack, etc.)
- Search intent classification (informational, navigational, commercial, transactional)
- CPC data (as commercial value proxy)

### Step 4: Clustering & Topic Mapping

1. Group keywords into topic clusters by semantic similarity
2. Identify primary keywords (highest volume in cluster) and supporting terms
3. Map clusters to existing pages where applicable
4. Flag content gaps — clusters with no matching page
5. Assign intent labels to each cluster

### Step 5: Prioritisation & Opportunity Scoring

Score each keyword/cluster using:
- **Volume** — monthly searches (weighted 25%)
- **Difficulty** — competition level (weighted 25%)
- **Relevance** — business alignment (weighted 30%)
- **Opportunity** — current position vs. potential (weighted 20%)

Categorise into:
- **Quick Wins** — Ranking positions 4–20 with low difficulty
- **Growth Targets** — High volume, medium difficulty, not yet ranking
- **Long-tail Expansion** — Low volume, low difficulty, high relevance
- **Strategic Plays** — High volume, high difficulty, long-term targets

### Step 6: Deliverable Assembly

1. Compile findings into the PRD-specified format
2. Include executive summary with top 10 opportunities
3. Provide full keyword list with all data columns
4. Add recommended content briefs for top 5 content gaps
5. Include competitor keyword overlap analysis

### Step 7: Quality Assurance

- Verify all data points are current (within 30 days)
- Check keyword-to-page mappings are accurate
- Ensure recommendations align with client's stated goals
- Peer review by a second team member before delivery
