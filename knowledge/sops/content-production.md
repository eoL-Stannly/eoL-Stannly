# SOP: Content Strategy & Production

**Owner:** Craig (SEO Director — Content Strategy)
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-02-12

---

## Purpose

Define the standard procedure for developing SEO content strategies and producing optimised content deliverables. Ensures all content is keyword-targeted, user-focused, and aligned with E-E-A-T principles.

## Scope

Covers content audits, content calendars, content briefs, on-page optimisation recommendations, and full content production for blog posts, landing pages, and pillar content.

## Prerequisites

- Completed keyword research (see Keyword Research SOP)
- Client brief with brand voice guidelines
- Access to existing content inventory/CMS
- Target audience personas (if available)

## Procedure

### Step 1: Content Audit & Gap Analysis

1. Crawl the client's existing content inventory
2. Map existing content to target keyword clusters
3. Score existing content on:
   - Keyword targeting effectiveness
   - Content quality and depth
   - Freshness (last updated date)
   - Performance metrics (traffic, rankings, engagement)
4. Identify gaps: keyword clusters with no supporting content
5. Identify cannibalisation: multiple pages targeting same cluster

### Step 2: Content Strategy Development

1. Define content pillars aligned with keyword clusters
2. Create topic cluster map: pillar pages → cluster content → supporting articles
3. Prioritise new content based on:
   - Keyword opportunity score (from keyword research)
   - Business impact potential
   - Content production effort estimate
   - Competitive landscape assessment
4. Build a 3-month content calendar with:
   - Content titles and target keywords
   - Content type (blog, landing page, guide, etc.)
   - Assigned writer/producer
   - Target publish date
   - Internal linking targets

### Step 3: Content Brief Creation

For each piece of content, produce a brief containing:
1. **Target keyword** and secondary keywords
2. **Search intent** and SERP analysis (what's currently ranking)
3. **Recommended word count** based on top-ranking competition
4. **Content outline** with H2/H3 structure
5. **Key points to cover** based on SERP feature analysis
6. **Internal linking targets** (existing pages to link to/from)
7. **E-E-A-T requirements** (expert quotes, data sources, author bio)
8. **CTA and conversion goals**

### Step 4: Content Production

1. Write content following the brief specifications
2. Ensure on-page SEO elements:
   - Title tag (60 chars, primary keyword)
   - Meta description (155 chars, compelling with keyword)
   - H1 tag matches target intent
   - H2/H3 hierarchy uses related keywords
   - Image alt text is descriptive and keyword-relevant
   - Internal links placed naturally (3–5 per 1000 words)
3. Apply E-E-A-T signals:
   - Expert author attribution
   - Data citations and sources
   - First-hand experience markers
   - Trust signals (methodology, credentials)

### Step 5: Content Optimisation Review

1. Run content through optimisation checklist:
   - Keyword density within acceptable range (1–2%)
   - All target keywords present in first 100 words
   - FAQ schema opportunities identified
   - Featured snippet optimisation (paragraph, list, or table format)
   - Readability score appropriate for target audience
2. Check for cannibalisation against existing content
3. Verify all links are functional
4. Confirm content matches specified word count ±10%

### Step 6: Quality Assurance

- Fact-check all claims and data points
- Ensure brand voice consistency
- Peer review for SEO best practices
- Verify E-E-A-T compliance
- Check for duplicate or thin content issues
