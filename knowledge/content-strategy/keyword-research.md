# Keyword Research & Search Intent

## Overview
Keyword research is the foundation of SEO content strategy. It identifies what your target audience searches for and how to align content with their intent.

## Search Intent Types

### Informational
- User wants to learn something: "what is technical SEO", "how to improve page speed"
- Best served by: blog posts, guides, tutorials, videos
- Often triggers featured snippets, People Also Ask

### Navigational
- User looking for a specific site/page: "GSC login", "Ahrefs pricing"
- Usually dominated by the brand itself
- Optimize brand pages and key landing pages

### Commercial Investigation
- User comparing options: "best SEO tools 2025", "Semrush vs Ahrefs"
- Best served by: comparison pages, reviews, listicles
- High value for affiliate and SaaS content

### Transactional
- User ready to take action: "buy Screaming Frog license", "hire SEO agency"
- Best served by: product pages, service pages, pricing pages
- Highest conversion intent

## Research Process

### 1. Seed Keywords
- Start with core topics relevant to the business
- Use client interviews, sales team insights, competitor analysis
- Think in terms of topics, not just individual keywords

### 2. Keyword Expansion
- **Tools**: Ahrefs Keywords Explorer, Semrush Keyword Magic, Google Keyword Planner
- Expand via: related terms, questions, long-tail variations, modifier patterns
- Mine GSC for existing queries with impressions but low CTR (quick wins)
- Use People Also Ask and autocomplete for question-based content ideas

### 3. Keyword Metrics
- **Search Volume**: Monthly average searches (note seasonality)
- **Keyword Difficulty (KD)**: Competition level for ranking
- **CPC**: Indicates commercial value
- **SERP Features**: What types of results appear (maps, images, videos, PAA)
- **Trend**: Is interest growing or declining (Google Trends)

### 4. Keyword Clustering
- Group semantically related keywords into topic clusters
- One page should target a cluster, not a single keyword
- Map clusters to search intent and funnel stage
- Identify pillar topics and supporting subtopics

## Prioritization Framework
- **Quick wins**: High impressions + low position (positions 5-20) in GSC
- **High value**: High volume + high CPC + achievable difficulty
- **Strategic**: Low volume but exact match to service offering
- **Content gaps**: Keywords competitors rank for but you don't
