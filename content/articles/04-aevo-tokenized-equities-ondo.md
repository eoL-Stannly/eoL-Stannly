## Answer up front

Six Ondo Finance tokenized stocks went live on Aevo on **7 August 2026**: **NVDAon, TSLAon, SPYon,
QQQon, HOODon and GOOGLon**. Aevo listed **matching perpetual contracts for all six at the same time**.

That simultaneity is the whole story. Spot and perp launching together, in one margin account, means
a trader could run a **delta-neutral position on a real-world equity from the first hour of listing** —
hold NVDAon, short the NVDA perp, collect the funding spread. On a venue that lists spot first and
perps later, that trade does not exist until the second listing.

It is Aevo's first integration of tokenized real-world equities.

## The market this is entering is growing violently

Tokenized equity perps went from a curiosity to a real market during 2026. The numbers are large and
the sources do not fully agree, which is worth showing rather than hiding.

**Q1 2026.** Tokenized versions of traditional assets reached **$30.7B in weekly volume**, about
**1.72% of the crypto derivatives market**. Stock perpetual swaps specifically grew **908% to roughly
$4.9B**. Stock perp open interest hit **$2.25B** — while total crypto market cap fell 20% over the
same quarter.

**Later in 2026.** Weekly stock-linked perp volume across centralised exchanges reached
**$141.84B**, described as roughly a **79-fold** increase since the start of the year. Weekly open
interest climbed to **$8.29B from $89M**, a **93-fold** rise.

**April 2026.** Tokenized perpetual swaps hit **$31B in weekly volume** on commodities volatility,
per CoinDesk.

Two things to flag. The $30.7B and $141.84B figures measure different scopes — one is tokenized
assets broadly, the other is stock-linked perps on centralised venues — so they are not a clean
before-and-after. And every one of these numbers is dwarfed by the comparison that matters: **US
equity markets turn over about $1.1 trillion per day**. Weekly on-chain volume is a rounding error
against daily traditional volume.

The growth rate is extraordinary. The absolute size is still small.

## Why 24/7 price discovery is the underrated part

Tokenized equity perps trade when the stock market is shut. That is not just a convenience feature.

When traditional sessions close, tokenized perps continue trading and **drive independent price
discovery**. Reporting on the 2026 market describes them functioning as a **leading indicator for the
next day's equity open**.

Think about what that means. A market that exists mostly because crypto traders wanted equity exposure
has started producing information that traditional markets read. Overnight news — an earnings leak, a
geopolitical event, a chip export rule — now has a venue to price into before the opening bell.

Growth in 2026 was reported as strongest in semiconductor and technology stocks, which is exactly where
overnight headline risk concentrates.

## The risks that come with the wrapper

**Backing is a claim, not a share.** One-to-one backing means an issuer holds the underlying. You own a
token representing that. You are exposed to Ondo's custody, attestation and redemption process. This is
issuer credit risk layered on top of equity risk.

**No shareholder rights.** A tokenized share is economic exposure. Voting rights and direct dividend
treatment generally do not pass through. Check the specific terms.

**Weekend gap risk cuts both ways.** A perp that trades through the weekend can move a long way from
Friday's close with thinner liquidity to absorb it. Leverage plus a gap plus a thin book is how
liquidations cluster.

**Regulatory status is unsettled.** Tokenized equities sit in an area regulators are actively working
through, and availability varies by jurisdiction.

## FAQ

**Which tokenized stocks are on Aevo?**
Six Ondo Finance assets: NVDAon (NVIDIA), TSLAon (Tesla), SPYon (S&P 500 ETF), QQQon (Nasdaq-100 ETF),
HOODon (Robinhood) and GOOGLon (Alphabet).

**When did they launch?**
7 August 2026. It was Aevo's first listing of tokenized real-world equities.

**Can I trade perps on them?**
Yes. Aevo launched matching perpetual contracts for all six at the same time as the spot assets.

**What is the delta-neutral trade everyone mentions?**
Hold the tokenized stock and short its matching perp from the same account. The equity exposure nets to
roughly zero and you are left with the funding spread.

**Are these real shares?**
No. Each token carries one-to-one backing by the underlying equity, held by the issuer. You hold a
token representing exposure, not a registered share, and shareholder rights generally do not pass through.

**Does it cost gas to move assets in?**
No. Aevo's zero-gas bridge transfers Ondo assets from Ethereum mainnet to Aevo Chain without
transaction fees.

**Can I trade these when the stock market is closed?**
Yes, and that is the point. Tokenized perps trade continuously and have been observed driving
independent price discovery outside traditional hours.

**How big is the tokenized stock perp market?**
Large in growth, small in absolute terms. Reported figures for 2026 range from $30.7B weekly for
tokenized assets broadly in Q1 to $141.84B weekly for stock-linked perps on centralised exchanges later
in the year. US equity markets trade about $1.1 trillion per day for comparison.

**Do tokenized stock perps predict the next day's open?**
Reporting on the 2026 market describes them acting as a leading indicator for next-day equity opens,
because they keep pricing overnight news while traditional venues are closed.

**What are the main risks?**
Issuer and custody risk on the wrapper, no shareholder rights, weekend gap risk against thinner
liquidity, and unsettled regulatory treatment that varies by jurisdiction.
