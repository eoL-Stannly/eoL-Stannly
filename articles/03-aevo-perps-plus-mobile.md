## Why a mobile release is a real story and not a changelog entry

Most "now on mobile" announcements are filler. This one has a specific argument behind it, and it is worth stating plainly rather than assuming.

Protection is time-sensitive. The value of a downside floor is highest at the moment you are away from a desk and the market moves. A protection feature that only exists on desktop protects you during the hours you were least exposed to being caught out.

There is a second, structural point. Aevo's whole 2026 product direction was **removing the interface barrier between retail traders and options**. Mobile is where retail derivatives trading actually happens. Shipping a one-tap options overlay to desktop only would have been shipping it to the wrong audience.

That reframes "platform parity" from a box-tick into the delivery step for the strategy.

## The context the launch coverage leaves out

Three facts materially affect whether this release is usable by a given reader. None appear in the syndicated press release.

**1. Geographic exclusion is broad.** U.S. and U.K. persons are excluded from the mobile app. Those are two of the largest English-language retail trading populations. A significant share of anyone reading an English-language article about this product cannot use it.

**2. Aevo has faced active regulatory enforcement.** The Philippine Securities and Exchange Commission moved against Aevo on **21 April 2026** over operating without local licences, alleging solicitation of deposits and investment services to Philippine residents without authorisation. Availability in a given jurisdiction can change.

**3. The product surface is narrow.** PERPS+ covers BTC and ETH. If your book is anything else, mobile parity does not reach your positions.

## FAQ

**Is PERPS+ available on the Aevo mobile app?**
Yes, since July 2026. All three protection modes are available with one-tap execution.

**Which assets does mobile PERPS+ cover?**
BTC and ETH perpetual futures, the same as the desktop release.

**Can I use the Aevo app in the United States or the United Kingdom?**
No. The app is stated as not available to U.S. or U.K. persons.

**Where do I download it?**
The App Store and Google Play, subject to the availability restrictions above.

**Does the mobile version have fewer features than desktop?**
Aevo's stated position is full parity — mobile mirrors desktop feature for feature. Verify anything you depend on inside the app.

**Does using PERPS+ on mobile cost more than on desktop?**
No pricing difference is stated in any available source. Premium and trading fees are properties of the structure, not the device.

**How large is Aevo?**
Launch materials cite more than $10 billion in options volume since 2020. Separate third-party summaries have cited monthly trading volumes above $15 billion and roughly 250,000 active traders as of 2025. Volume figures for decentralised venues vary by methodology — treat them as order-of-magnitude, not audited.

**Is Aevo regulated?**
Aevo is a decentralised exchange, not a licensed broker in most jurisdictions. It faced enforcement from the Philippine SEC in April 2026. Check your local position before trading.
